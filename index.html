THE HEART OF THE PROJECT ✳

There is always more to the story.

Beyond Silence explores how social, emotional, and economic pressures may affect children’s focus, participation, confidence, and everyday experiences. Our goal is not to label children—it is to understand what they may be going through and help create environments where they feel safe enough to speak.

THE CHALLENGELook closer ↙

When pressure becomes hard to explain.

Some children experience challenges that are easy to miss from the outside. When support is limited, these pressures may affect how they learn, connect with others, and manage daily life.

✳

Bullying & exclusion

Being mocked, left out, or made to feel unsafe around peers.

◉

Comparison & pressure

Feeling that their worth depends on performance or matching others.

⌂

Economic barriers

Unequal access to learning resources, activities, and opportunities.

♡

Limited communication

Finding it difficult to share worries or feel heard by trusted adults.

i

Important: These experiences do not affect every child in the same way. Distraction or silence alone cannot tell us what a child is experiencing. Listening with care is a starting point—not a diagnosis.

REAL-WORLD PERSPECTIVES ✳

User interviews.

Different ages. Different perspectives. One shared need: to be heard.

✳

We conducted more interviews than the three shown here. We selected these three because they were especially helpful and represented our three main user-target perspectives.

SM

Shams Mohamed

Kids Programming Instructor

EDUCATOR
Q

What do you think is the biggest challenge displayed by children today?

A: The lack of focus and emotional stability. Many kids are distracted because of pressure from their surroundings, and it affects their learning and creativity.

Q

How can we support them better?

A: By giving them safe spaces, encouraging them, and providing activities that build confidence and problem-solving skills.

EM

Enass Mohamed

A Mother of 2 Children

PARENT
Q

What challenges do you face as a parent?

A: It’s hard to see my children stressed or comparing themselves to others. Sometimes I feel they don’t talk about what they’re going through, and I’m not always sure how to help them.

Q

What do you think they need most?

A: They need to feel heard, supported, and to have access to positive role models and safe environments.

RY

Rima Yousef

9 Years Old

CHILD
Q

What makes you feel upset at school or home?

A: When someone makes fun of me or compares me to others. I also feel sad when I can’t talk to my parents about what’s bothering me.

Q

What would help you feel better?

A: I want someone to listen to me, and I want to have more friends and activities. I also want to be more confident in myself.

♡ Children’s perspectives should always be collected and shared with appropriate guardian consent, child assent, and respect for privacy and safety.
